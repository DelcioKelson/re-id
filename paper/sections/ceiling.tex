\subsection{Setup}
\label{sec:setup}

Ten matchers use crack-centred boxes with a common mask: SIFT/ORB with RANSAC inliers, SuperGlue/LoFTR with indoor weights, five embeddings and OSNet with cosine on L2 crops, all without \textsc{CrackID} tuning (YOLOv8: layer-9 \texttt{yolov8n.pt}; sizes/checkpoints in code).
Context expands the box $1\times$ per side; erased views inpaint via Telea after 5~px dilation (artefacts possible; sibling/neighbour handling unstated).
Only OSNet/CLIP tested, no crack-only/wall-only views.
\regonly{} scores a constant on registered pairs.
Appearance sees tight crops, geometry full photographs.
Missing: full-photo descriptors (DINOv2, NetVLAD~\cite{arandjelovic2016netvlad}), LightGlue~\cite{lindenberger2023lightglue}, matcher-fed Chamfer (see repo for timings; sessions not load-controlled, no ranking claimed).

\subsection{Result: ranking spreads, decisions stay near the constant baseline}

Ranking spreads while thresholded decisions stay near the constant predictor (Table~\ref{tab:main}).
Rank-1 0.352--0.654; mAP to 0.520; crop DIR to 0.429.
Oracle $F_{1}^{*}$ 0.305--0.359; validated $F_{1}^{@v}$ 0.293--0.351 (excess $-0.012$ to $+0.012$ except LoFTR $+0.046$; ORB/SIFT/SuperGlue accept everything at 0.305).
AUROC/AUPRC, DIR--FAR curves, thresholds and wall-clustered CIs need archived matrices; leave-one-wall-out over 17 walls should replace the 6/11 split.
Part of the drop is prevalence shift (0.292 to 0.180), not only appearance.
With 19 identities on 11 walls, we claim no fine-grained ranking or separability from chance.

\subsection{Is it the crack, or the wall behind it?}
\label{sec:controls}

For OSNet, enlarging the crop raises DIR from 0.168 to 0.404 at FAR\,0.1; erasing the crack retains DIR at 0.393 (Rank-1: 0.711 vs 0.775), suggesting that location-specific context carries much of the signal.
For CLIP, DIR rises from 0.143 to 0.311 with context and 0.286 after erasure, showing a similar pattern.
Deltas are point estimates without CIs.
The $1\times$ box mostly adds neighbouring cracks, possibly sibling fragments (45\% merged), so the signal source is context around cracks rather than wall texture alone.
Crack-only and wall-only views on additional backbones with CIs would sharpen this interpretation.
$F_{1}^{*}$ stays 0.307--0.361.
