\section{Related Work}

Crack analysis targets detection and
segmentation~\cite{zou2012cracktree,liu2019deepcrack,eisenbach2017gaps}; its
datasets annotate single images and consolidations
inherit that scope~\cite{kulkarni2022crackseg9k,benz2024omnicrack30k}, and multi-view pipelines
fuse one inspection into a surface or
segmentation~\cite{benz2025crackstructures} without instance correspondence. Structural-health monitoring
compares cracks over time with related
machinery~\cite{parente2022monitoring,harb2025multitemporal,sun2025perspective,nec2019guide},
but identity is \emph{given by construction} (fixed camera, marked
location): they measure a known crack rather than deciding association under an open-set protocol, which is our target.

Object re-identification~\cite{qian2024reidtrends} with open-set
identification~\cite{liao2014openset} is our formal setting;
keypoint matching~\cite{lowe2004sift,rublee2011orb}, learned correspondence
(SuperGlue~\cite{sarlin2020superglue}, LoFTR~\cite{sun2021loftr}), and
region embeddings~\cite{dosovitskiy2021vit,touvron2021deit,radford2021clip,zhou2019osnet,oquab2024dinov2}
apply to crack crops, all benchmarked here zero-shot without \textsc{CrackID} fine-tuning (YOLOv8 row: layer-$9$ \texttt{yolov8n.pt} features~\cite{jocher2023ultralytics}; LightGlue~\cite{lindenberger2023lightglue} and NetVLAD~\cite{arandjelovic2016netvlad} left as future retrieval baselines).
Vehicle-damage re-identification across claims~\cite{maiano2023antifraud} mirrors our open-set protocol; the rest of that literature covers detection/severity rather than association~\cite{hasan2025review,perez2024assessment,mallios2023multiview}.
Aligning captures then reasoning about presence versus absence is change
detection~\cite{sakurada2015changedetection,alcantarilla2018street} with classical planar
registration~\cite{hartley2004mvg,fischler1987ransac,barath2019magsac} and ECC fallback~\cite{evangelidis2008ecc}; our point is that alignment and matching targets should be \emph{disjoint}, with the homography driven by the wall because the crack is what is to be identified.
