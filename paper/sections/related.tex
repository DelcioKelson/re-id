\section{Related Work}

The analysis of cracks focuses on detection and segmentation for single images~\cite{zou2012cracktree,liu2019deepcrack,eisenbach2017gaps}; so do the consolidation studies~\cite{kulkarni2022crackseg9k,benz2024omnicrack30k}, and the multi-view pipelines combine one inspection into a surface or segmentation~\cite{benz2025crackstructures} without taking into account instance correspondence. Structural-health monitoring involves tracking cracks over time using fixed cameras and designated locations~\cite{parente2022monitoring,harb2025multitemporal,sun2025perspective,nec2019guide}, in which the identity of the cracks is \emph{assigned at the time of construction}: unlike in our open-set association task, they are dealing with a known crack.



The scenario involves object re-ID~\cite{qian2024reidtrends} with open-set identification~\cite{liao2014openset}: Scale-Invariant Feature Transform (SIFT)~\cite{lowe2004sift} and Oriented FAST and Rotated BRIEF (ORB)~\cite{rublee2011orb}, the learned correspondences such as SuperGlue~\cite{sarlin2020superglue} and LoFTR~\cite{sun2021loftr}, and the region embeddings~\cite{dosovitskiy2021vit,touvron2021deit,radford2021clip,zhou2019osnet,oquab2024dinov2} are all assessed in a zero-shot manner without \textsc{CrackID} fine-tuning (the YOLOv8 row: layer-9 \texttt{yolov8n.pt} features~\cite{jocher2023ultralytics}). LightGlue~\cite{lindenberger2023lightglue} and NetVLAD~\cite{arandjelovic2016netvlad} are mentioned as future retrieval baselines. Vehicle-damage re-ID across claims~\cite{maiano2023antifraud} follows the same open-set protocol as ours; related work focuses on detection and severity, not on association~\cite{hasan2025review,perez2024assessment,mallios2023multiview}. The approach of aligning first and then reasoning about presence or absence constitutes change detection~\cite{sakurada2015changedetection,alcantarilla2018street} using planar registration~\cite{hartley2004mvg,fischler1987ransac,barath2019magsac} and an Enhanced Correlation Coefficient (ECC) fallback~\cite{evangelidis2008ecc}; our key point is that the alignment and the matching targets must be \emph{disjoint}, with the homography being derived from the wall when the crack is identified.
