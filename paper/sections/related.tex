\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

Most crack-analysis research focuses on detecting or segmenting cracks in individual images~\cite{zou2012cracktree,liu2019deepcrack,eisenbach2017gaps}.
Consolidated datasets~\cite{kulkarni2022crackseg9k,benz2024omnicrack30k} retain this image-level formulation and do not provide identity annotations across observations.
Although multi-view pipelines establish geometric correspondences to fuse images acquired during a single inspection into surface models or aggregated segmentations~\cite{benz2025crackstructures}, they do not explicitly represent persistent crack identities across observations.
Structural-health monitoring tracks cracks over time using fixed cameras and designated locations~\cite{parente2022monitoring,harb2025multitemporal,sun2025perspective} or guided setups~\cite{nec2019guide}, where crack identities are assigned at construction time; unlike our open-set association task, they track known cracks.

The scenario involves object re-ID~\cite{qian2024reidtrends} with open-set identification~\cite{liao2014openset}:
SIFT~\cite{lowe2004sift} and ORB~\cite{rublee2011orb}, learned correspondences such as SuperGlue~\cite{sarlin2020superglue} and LoFTR~\cite{sun2021loftr}, and region embeddings~\cite{zhou2019osnet,dosovitskiy2021vit,touvron2021deit,radford2021clip,oquab2024dinov2} are all assessed in a zero-shot manner without \textsc{CrackID} fine-tuning (the YOLOv8 row: layer-9 \texttt{yolov8n.pt} features~\cite{jocher2023ultralytics}).
LightGlue~\cite{lindenberger2023lightglue} and NetVLAD~\cite{arandjelovic2016netvlad} are mentioned as future retrieval baselines.
Vehicle-damage re-ID across claims~\cite{maiano2023antifraud} follows the same open-set protocol as ours; related work focuses on detection and severity, not on association~\cite{hasan2025review,perez2024assessment,mallios2023multiview}.
The approach of aligning first and then reasoning about presence or absence constitutes change detection~\cite{sakurada2015changedetection,alcantarilla2018street} using planar registration~\cite{hartley2004mvg,fischler1987ransac,barath2019magsac} and an ECC fallback~\cite{evangelidis2008ecc}; our key point is that the alignment and the matching targets must be disjoint, with the homography being derived from the wall when the crack is identified.
