\section{Related Work}

\label{sec:related}



Most crack work detects/segments single photographs~\cite{zou2012cracktree,liu2019deepcrack,eisenbach2017gaps}. Consolidated sets~\cite{kulkarni2022crackseg9k,benz2024omnicrack30k} give no cross-photograph identities. Multi-view pipelines fuse one inspection via geometry~\cite{benz2025crackstructures} without persistent identities. Monitoring tracks fixed cameras~\cite{parente2022monitoring,harb2025multitemporal,sun2025perspective} or guides (patent~\cite{nec2019guide}) with identities at construction. Ours combines object re-ID~\cite{qian2024reidtrends} with open-set identification~\cite{liao2014openset}; ranking follows Market-1501~\cite{zheng2015market1501}.



Standard blocks are SIFT~\cite{lowe2004sift}, ORB~\cite{rublee2011orb}, SuperGlue~\cite{sarlin2020superglue} and LoFTR~\cite{sun2021loftr} (indoor weights), crop embeddings~\cite{dosovitskiy2021vit,touvron2021deit,radford2021clip,zhou2019osnet,oquab2024dinov2} and YOLOv8~\cite{jocher2023ultralytics}. Outdoor weights, LightGlue, dense matchers, full-photo descriptors (e.g.\ NetVLAD~\cite{arandjelovic2016netvlad}) and matcher-fed Chamfer are not tested. Vehicle damage~\cite{maiano2023antifraud} shares the open-set protocol; other damage work is detection/severity~\cite{hasan2025review,perez2024assessment,mallios2023multiview}. Align-first is change detection~\cite{sakurada2015changedetection,alcantarilla2018street} via planar registration~\cite{hartley2004mvg,fischler1987ransac,barath2019magsac} with ECC~\cite{evangelidis2008ecc}; spatial verification~\cite{philbin2007object,radenovic2018revisiting} is the precedent. The contribution is protocol/labels/controls, not the machinery. Location/BIM/markers/SfM are unevaluated, though the wall is given. No public crack re-ID benchmark found in our searches. Alignment (wall) and matching (crack) targets must be disjoint.
