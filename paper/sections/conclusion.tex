\section{Conclusion}

We built a dataset that makes crack re-identification testable:
\textsc{CrackID}, 140 photographs over 17 walls with 212 identities in the
frozen snapshot (6 validation walls, 11 test walls; wall18--wall19 excluded), its
annotation provenance ($45\%$ merged; $48\%$ on test) stated rather than assumed, with the
triage of unclaimed clicks complete ($107/1\,023$ naming no crack). All
accuracy rows are point estimates with no
wall-level intervals ($19$ matchable test identities, $n_Q{=}280$); we claim
the pattern, not the digits. The
benchmark's main finding is negative and structural: on the decision a
record requires, ten zero-shot matchers across four families buy between
$+0.000$ and $+0.054$ over the $0.305$ best-constant floor ($F_1^{*}$ $0.305$--$0.359$),
with eight of ten rows at or below the floor at the validation threshold ($F_1^{@v}$ $0.293$--$0.351$; LoFTR alone at $+0.046$).
Ordering a gallery and deciding an
identity are not the same capability. The difficulty is not crack
appearance alone: a geometric reference baseline that registers on wall texture
with cracks masked out and Chamfer-matches projected masks reaches
$\mathrm{DIR}@\mathrm{FAR}{=}0.1$ of $0.768$ against $0.429$ for the best
crop method and $0.404$ for the best
context method while answering $48\%$ of pairs, and a \coverage{} control
exploiting
that alignment without examining a crack scores $0.000$ on the open-set
metric ($0.544$ pairwise $F_1$ on its own $0.544$ floor).
Operationally, the open-set advantage comes from an aligned pair supporting
a \emph{licensed negative}, positive evidence of absence, where a crop
embedding can only report low similarity; whether that advantage is
independent of homography-based annotation remains unresolved and must be
read as provenance-conditioned. Two bounds
remain addressable: a real second capture session turns the benchmark from
viewpoint to cross-visit, and a second annotator plus a homography-free audit
subset would turn the single-annotator
triage into a measured agreement rate. The
$48\%$ coverage is also a deployment gap in its own right, not merely a
scoring issue: smooth-plaster walls can be categorically unanswerable.
Finally, the small single-seed synthetic revisit checkpoint reverses the headline ranking and
open-set result in favour of OSNet$+$context, while the distinct
\hybrid{} pipeline
retains only a narrow assignment-$F_1$ advantage; the present paper
therefore establishes a promising within-visit geometric reference, not a
solved re-identification component for a multi-visit ticket management backend.
