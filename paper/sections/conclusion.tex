\section{Conclusion}

We present \textsc{CrackID} for testing crack re-ID: 140 photos from 17 walls with 212 identities in the frozen snapshot (6 validation, 11 test; walls 18--19 excluded); annotation provenance is stated (45\% merged, 48\% of test from merges) and unclaimed-click triage is complete (107 of 1,023 as no crack). All accuracies are point estimates without wall-level intervals (19 matchable test identities, $n_{Q}{=}305$); we assert the pattern, not the digits.



The main result is negative and of a structural nature: when decisions have to be made, ten zero-shot matchers from four families end up between $+0.032$ and $+0.069$ above the $0.305$ best-constant floor ($F_{1}^{*}$ ranging from $0.337$ to $0.374$), with validation-calibrated decisions lying between $F_{1}^{@v}$ values of $0.331$ and $0.338$. Retrieving from a gallery is different from deciding. The criterion is not merely the appearance of cracks: a geometric reference that captures the wall texture even when cracks are masked and involves Chamfer-matches being projected achieves a DIR at FAR=0.1 of 0.768 compared to 0.384 for the best crop and 0.404 for the best context on 48\% of the pairs, whereas a \coverage{} control that uses the same alignment but omits the crack scores gives a score of 0.000 in the open-set (its pairwise $F_{1}$ is 0.544 on its own $0.544$ floor).



In terms of operation, the open-set gain is a \emph{licensed negative}—positive evidence of absence—since the crop embeddings exhibit only low similarity; it has not been proven to be independent of homography-based annotation and must therefore be regarded as being conditioned by provenance. Two shortcomings still remain: a genuine second session is needed to make the benchmark cover a cross-visit scenario, and a second annotator is required together with a homography-free audit subset for the purpose of measuring agreement. The 48\% coverage figure is in itself a shortcoming in terms of deployment because smooth plaster can be unanswerable. Lastly, the small single-seed synthetic revisit reverses the ranking mentioned in the headline in favour of OSNet+context, with the distinct \hybrid{} method maintaining only a small lead in assignment-$F_{1}$; this represents a within-visit geometric reference and not a multi-visit ticket-backend solution.
