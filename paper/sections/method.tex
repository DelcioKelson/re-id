\section{A Geometric Reference Baseline}
\label{sec:method}

The baseline tests whether crack locations on the wall drive the association: it aligns wall surfaces to a common frame and then compares the projected crack masks.
It assumes a roughly planar wall and uses no \textsc{CrackID} training.
The shared masks (trained on public crack data only; no \textsc{CrackID} wall in training) use instance extraction at 200~px and 5~px to keep inputs consistent across methods.
Masks are taken as given, and their error is not measured.

\subsection{Registration using wall texture}

For $I_a$, $I_b$, a homography $H$ ($x' \sim Hx$) is estimated from the surrounding wall texture with detected cracks dilated and excluded, so alignment is driven by surface features independently of the identity under test.
This independence is conditional on the masks and the succeeding stage, not guaranteed per pair.
Full detector settings (SIFT with CLAHE~\cite{zuiderveld1994clahe}, ratio test, robust estimation) are archived with the code.

\subsection{Registration cascade and validity checks}

Textured render generally provides background features, whereas smooth plaster does not.
A three-stage cascade is used: masked SIFT; if too few keypoints remain, unmasked SIFT (flagged, crack leakage occurs); finally, photometric ECC~\cite{evangelidis2008ecc}.
Candidate solutions are tested for plausibility (no mirrored or folded quads; scale within $[1/16,16]$; no inlier-count criterion).
We record the surviving stage per pair, and the inlier RMSE at that stage determines the comparison tolerance.
Absence of registration means lack of evidence, not that the cracks differ.

\subsection{Crack projection and geometric comparison}
\label{sec:chamfer}

Accepted registrations project instances from $I_a$ into $I_b$ and compare them using the symmetric mean Chamfer distance~\cite{barrow1977chamfer} as in \eqref{eq:chamfer}; this allows small misalignments by using IoU on narrow cracks.
Let $a,b$ be crack point sets and $d(p, b)=\min_{q \in b}\lVert p-q\rVert_2$:
\begin{equation}
D(a,b)=\frac{1}{2}\left[\frac{1}{|a|}\sum_{p \in a} d(p, b)+
\frac{1}{|b|}\sum_{q \in b} d(q, a)\right].
\label{eq:chamfer}
\end{equation}
We reject candidates that are far away.
Chamfer coverage (fraction within the per-pair RMSE, distance clamped at $[2,16]$~px, default 6~px) is the scale-free equivalent.

\subsection{Correspondence decisions and limits of spatial evidence}

For each pair of photographs we carry out one-to-one matching using the Hungarian method~\cite{kuhn1955hungarian} at the validation threshold; unregistered pairs score $-\infty$ (Sec.~\ref{sec:protocol}).
Alignment of an image with an empty wall acts as evidence of absence; missed detections, out-of-frame areas, and registration failures remain unresolved.
The reference targets show no change in damage; performance is determined by the surface and by the origin of the label.
