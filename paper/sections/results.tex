\section{Results and Discussion}
\label{sec:results}

\subsection{Main comparison and the open-set gap}
Geometric Rank-1 reaches $0.952$ vs crop best $0.654$ (ViT-B/16 and OSNet tied) and mAP $0.886$ vs $0.520$ (OSNet) (\emph{scored} $0.48$ vs $1.00$). Conditioning on coverage as excess over \coverage{}, ranking gains stay large, but the open-set number is what coverage cannot explain: $\mathrm{DIR}@\mathrm{FAR}{=}0.1$ of $0.768$ vs $0.429$ (SuperGlue), $0.404$ (best context OSNet$+$ctx1), $0.038$ chance and $0.000$ for \coverage{}. Crop similarities on self-similar hairlines rarely support rejection, while an aligned empty projection gives a licensed negative. Compute does not explain the gap (LoFTR $8.64$\,s vs OSNet $0.05$\,s per query; Reg $27.9$\,s per query, $26.0$\,s per image pair; timings relative only). RQ2 (geometric part): alignment adds identification beyond coverage alone, conditional on success, surface, and provenance. All numbers are point estimates on $19$ matchable identities.

\subsection{Partial coverage, gap structure, and failures}
\label{sec:coverage}
Crop methods score $100\%$ of pairs, geometry $48\%$; unscored pairs are charged negative/rank-last. Censoring penalises ranking but subsidises pairwise $F_1$: the scorable subset keeps $100\%$ of positives but only $36.8\%$ of negatives (prevalence $0.180{\to}0.373$, floor $0.305{\to}0.544$). \coverage{}---constant on registered pairs without inspecting cracks---thus scores $0.544$ pairwise $F_1$, $0.533$ mAP and $0.515$ Rank-1: much of the ranking/pairwise margin is \emph{which pairs align}. The open-set lead ($0.768$ vs $0.000$) is the exception we lead with. Failures are wall-dependent (two test walls $0\%$; full per-wall table in supplement), so aggregates are not uniform across surfaces. All $280$ answerable queries are answered by the adjacent frame, so headline ranking is short-baseline correspondence; gap sweeps ($280{\to}105$ at gap $3$) leave Rank-1 flat on a shrinking pool rather than proving irrelevance. Registration follows image sharpness over frame gap (wall-clustered Wald $z{=}5.11$, $p{=}3.3{\times}10^{-7}$ vs $-2.78$, $p{=}5.4{\times}10^{-3}$; details in supplement); one sharpness gate ($\geq 10$) raises registration to $56.1\%$ on the filtered set at the cost of one wall, suggesting capture-quality guidance, not full-population coverage.

\subsection{Ablations and cost}
\label{sec:chamferablation}
Coverage (scale-free) costs $0.240$ mAP and $0.103$ Rank-1 versus Chamfer distance, with DIR nearly unchanged ($+0.014$): the comparison rule moves ranking, not decisions. Disabling crack exclusion leaves ranking/pairwise unchanged but raises DIR by $+0.057$; masking is retained for evidence independence, not observed accuracy here. ECC adds ${\sim}+0.03$ Rank-1 / $+0.02$ mAP with DIR unchanged. Cost is $26.0$\,s per image pair ($7817.3$\,s over $301$ test pairs, i.e.\ $27.9$\,s per query; Chamfer-only thereafter), about $3{\times}$ LoFTR per query and more than two orders above the cheapest embedding; absolute timings are workstation-dependent and SuperGlue/LoFTR were timed in a separate run.

\subsection{A synthetic revisit protocol}
\label{sec:hybridbench}
