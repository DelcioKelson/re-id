\subsection{Setup}
\label{sec:setup}

Ten matchers operate on crack-centered boxes taken from the shared masks. SIFT, ORB, SuperGlue, and LoFTR are scored by RANSAC inlier counts, with indoor weights for SuperGlue and LoFTR; the five embedding models (listed in Table~\ref{tab:main}) and OSNet are compared by cosine similarity of L2-normalized crop embeddings. None is fine-tuned on \textsc{CrackID}. For YOLOv8~\cite{jocher2023ultralytics}, we use layer-9 features of \texttt{yolov8n.pt}; model sizes and checkpoints are in the code.
Context views extend the box by one width and height on each side (three times as wide and tall); OSNet with this crop is OSNet+ctx.
In erased views, the crack is dilated by 5~px and inpainted with Telea's method~\cite{telea2004inpaint}; artifacts are possible, and the treatment of other fragments and neighboring cracks is not described.
Context and erasure views were tested only for OSNet and CLIP; no crack-only or wall-only views were evaluated.
The appearance baselines operate on tight crops, whereas the geometric reference uses full photographs.
We do not evaluate full-photograph descriptors (DINOv2 on whole photographs, NetVLAD~\cite{arandjelovic2016netvlad}), LightGlue~\cite{lindenberger2023lightglue}, or matcher-fed Chamfer (Chamfer after registering with a learned matcher).
Runtimes are in the code repository; sessions were not load-controlled, so they do not rank methods.

\subsection{Ranking quality versus thresholded decisions}
\label{sec:baselines}

Ranking quality varies widely, while thresholded decisions stay near the constant baseline (Table~\ref{tab:main}).
Rank-1 ranges from 0.352 to 0.654, mAP reaches 0.520, and crop DIR reaches 0.429.
The oracle-threshold $F_1$ ranges from 0.305 to 0.359 and the validation-calibrated $F_1^{@v}$ from 0.293 to 0.351. Relative to the 0.305 constant baseline, the validation-calibrated excess is between $-0.012$ and $+0.012$ for every method except LoFTR ($+0.046$); ORB, SIFT, and SuperGlue accept all pairs at the validation-calibrated threshold ($F_1 = 0.305$).
AUROC, average precision (AP), DIR--FAR curves, and wall-clustered confidence intervals require archived score matrices, so we do not report them; leave-one-wall-out evaluation over all 17 walls would be preferable to the 6/11 split (Section~\ref{sec:limitations}).
The constant-baseline $F_1$ falls from 0.452 (validation) to 0.305 (test) because prevalence shifts from 0.292 to 0.180, so absolute $F_1$ values are not comparable across splits.
With 19 matchable identities on 11 walls, we do not claim a fine-grained ranking or separability from chance.

\subsection{Crack appearance versus wall context}
\label{sec:controls}

For OSNet, context raises DIR from 0.168 to 0.404 at FAR\,0.1; erasing the crack leaves DIR at 0.393 (Rank-1 0.711 vs.\ 0.775 with context), suggesting that location-specific context carries much of the signal.
For CLIP, DIR rises from 0.143 to 0.311 with context and to 0.286 after erasure, showing a similar pattern.
These differences are point estimates without confidence intervals.
The context box mostly adds neighboring cracks, possibly sibling fragments (45\% of identities result from merges, Section~\ref{sec:annotation}); the signal likely comes from context around cracks rather than wall texture alone.
Crack-only and wall-only views on more backbones with confidence intervals would sharpen this.
Across these controls, $F_{1}^{*}$ stays within 0.307--0.361.
